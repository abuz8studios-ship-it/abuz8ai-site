How we hire an AI.

Not a clone of anyone’s book. Five rules we actually run. Paid edition only after Stripe is a real button and the PDF is a real file.

1. Don’t chat. Hire.

A tab that forgets you is a tool. A hire has a name, a job, memory, tools, and a done-check.

2. One job each.

Amanecer: X + site + this company face. Zait: OpenClaw. Qadir: Hermes hands. OS: the desktop product. Rescue: the only paid offer.

3. Done means a check.

URL, test, file, or receipt. If the model says “done” and nothing was checked, it is not done.

4. Email and money stay human.

Email is never a command channel. No unsupervised spend. Approval before send.

5. Sell the hole, not the stack.

People pay to unstick a project, not to hear “agent OS.” Our hole: 48-hour autopsy, $1,500.
