Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 20, 2026 · ABUZ8

The short version: we collect as little as possible, we don't sell your personal information, and we use privacy-conscious analytics and advertising partners to support free content.

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information. Advertising may appear on free content pages so ABUZ8 can keep publishing useful tools, news, and guides.

1. What we collect

We keep this deliberately small:

  • Privacy-first analytics. We use analytics to understand aggregate traffic, popular pages, and performance issues. Where possible, we prefer cookieless and non-identifying measurement.
  • Advertising signals. If ads are enabled, advertising partners such as Google may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure, and improve ads. You can manage ad personalization through your browser settings and Google ad controls.
  • Email, only if you give it. If you join an early-access waitlist, we store the email you submit and which product you were interested in, so we can notify you. That's it. You can ask us to delete it anytime.
  • Tool inputs. What you type or upload into a tool is used only to generate the result you asked for (see "AI processing" below).
  • Payment data. Purchases are handled by Stripe. We never receive or store your full card number — Stripe does that securely on their side.

2. What we don't collect or sell

We do not sell visitor lists, payment details, uploaded files, prompts, tool inputs, or account data. We do not read files on your device beyond what you explicitly upload to a tool.

3. AI processing — local-first

A core principle of how we build: AI work runs locally on our own infrastructure or in your browser wherever possible, rather than being routed through third-party AI vendors who may retain your inputs. When a tool processes an image, document, or prompt, that input is used to produce your result and is not added to a training set or sold.

4. How we use what we collect

  • To run and improve the tools and the site.
  • To notify you about early access if you asked us to.
  • To fulfill and support a purchase you made.
  • To understand, in aggregate, what's useful so we build more of it.

5. Third parties we rely on

We use Cloudflare for hosting, analytics, and infrastructure; Stripe for payments; and may use Google advertising services to support free public content. These providers process data under their own policies and service terms.

6. Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. For waitlist emails, deletion is immediate on request. Email support@abuz8ai.com and we'll handle it.

7. Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. Material changes will be made clear on the site.

9. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: support@abuz8ai.com.
