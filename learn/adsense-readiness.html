How We Prepare a Website for Ad Monetization

Ad monetization starts with trust. A site needs original content, stable navigation, clear legal pages, accessible contact information, and enough useful material that visitors have a reason to stay.

Original content

Thin pages do not build durable traffic. ABUZ8's content strategy is to publish practical guides, tool explainers, AI news summaries, and build logs that help people understand local AI agents and useful automation.

Clean structure

Search engines need crawlable pages with clear titles, descriptions, internal links, and a sitemap. Visitors need the same thing: a way to move from news to tools to tutorials without confusion.

Legal and trust pages

Privacy, terms, contact, robots.txt, sitemap.xml, and ads.txt are part of the foundation. They do not create revenue by themselves, but they reduce friction when applying to advertising platforms.

Ad placement

The best ad strategy does not bury the content. Ads should support the page, not replace it. Useful placements include in-article units, sidebar units on desktop layouts, and responsive blocks between major sections.
