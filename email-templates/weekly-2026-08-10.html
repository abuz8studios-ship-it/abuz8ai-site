What we learned

Our own link check passed 180 of 180 pages. Every button was present, styled and clickable. It was still wrong.

The check asked is the button there. It never asked does the destination exist, and does it capture anything. Forty-nine of those buttons pointed at a homepage section that had been removed. Green across the board while nearly fifty calls to action quietly went nowhere.

A presence check is not a quality check. We rewrote ours to follow every link to its endpoint and confirm something is actually listening on the other side.
