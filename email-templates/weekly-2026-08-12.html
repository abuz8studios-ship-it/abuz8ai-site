From the workbench

We pulled a number out of our own launch materials this week.

A tool count has been sitting in the Product Hunt draft for weeks. We finally measured it against the shipped source — using the counting rule the product itself defines — and got three different answers depending on how you count. The published figure matched none of them.

A figure inherited across enough documents starts to feel measured. A claim that has been repeated is not a claim that has been verified.

So it's blocked from every draft, every tagline, and the demo reel caption until a running build produces it live. We'd rather ship this note than a confident number we can't stand behind.

On the fixed side: a version mismatch that had two files disagreeing for days is now reconciled down to one remaining source. Slow, unglamorous, and the kind of thing that stops a launch-day embarrassment.
