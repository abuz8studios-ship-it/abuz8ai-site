Shipped this week
ABUZ8 OS v2.4.0 — the see-hear-talk loop. It streams its answers token-by-token, listens on a wake word with hands-free voice and barge-in, and senses presence and attention from the webcam — with the vision model running fully offline, so your face never leaves the machine. Gestures act, too: open palm wakes it, a fist stops it.
I fixed a regression I'd shipped. When I promoted a cleaner shell for its layout, streaming and the webcam awareness quietly stopped working — I never audited the new shell for feature parity. Found it, ported the old implementations back in, verified both live. Parity is a feature; dropping it is a bug in a nicer coat.
Dead panes, now wired. A tracer across the whole OS found the backends were real but the UI never called them. The editor now saves and shows a revertible colorized diff; the activity feed and sessions list are live. Half of "build the feature" was just connecting the wire.
The media engine runs itself. The OS starts its own image/video pipeline (100+ models on disk, FLUX included) and generates on your GPU — no separate app to launch, nothing sent to a cloud.
7 new guides — LoRA adapters explained, how to run FLUX locally, Ollama vs LM Studio, LLM quantization, plus AI agents for restaurants and podcasters. Read them →
Still stuck (the honest part)
Same drag as last week: auto-posting is down, so this log and the new guides keep going out by hand — the content stacks up faster than it reaches anyone, and that's the biggest bottleneck on the whole thing. Revenue's honest too: the store works, the rails are proven, nobody's bought yet. And the voice, specifically, is live but running on a neural fallback engine while I finish tuning a higher-quality one — "works, on the fallback" is the accurate sentence, so that's the one I'm using. ABUZ8 OS is early access and still hardening. No "it's all done" theater.
Next week

The build was never the bottleneck — reach is. Focus: reconnect the posting pipeline, capture a fresh demo that actually shows the new voice-and-vision loop, and point real traffic at the live tools and the new guides. Less building, more shipping what's built.
