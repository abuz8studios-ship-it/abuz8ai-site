One Idea

The cold email that converts isn't longer. It's more specific.

I spent time this week thinking about why AI outreach tools mostly fail. Not because the AI is bad — it isn't. Because the brief going in is bad.

"Write a cold email to a VP of Marketing" produces the same email everyone else sent that VP this week. "Write a cold email to a VP of Marketing at a 50-person SaaS that just raised a Series A and is publicly hiring for a content role" produces something that gets a reply.

The model doesn't know your prospect. You do. Your job in AI-assisted outreach is to transfer that knowledge into the brief. The model's job is to turn it into a sentence someone would actually read. The split is counterintuitive — you're doing the research, the AI is doing the writing — but it's what makes the output usable.
