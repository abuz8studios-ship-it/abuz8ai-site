I kept pulling one thread: you're not paying for AI, you're paying by the meter. This week I wrote the honest local twin for five of them, plus a plain-English explainer of the meter itself.

The honest part
Same truth as last week, because pretending otherwise would be theater: building was the easy 90%. The sovereign OS runs, the tools work, the rails are proven — and revenue is still exactly $0, zero charges ever. That's a distribution problem, not a product one. Auto-posting is still down, so every guide and this very email goes out by hand and stacks up faster than it reaches anyone. The store's payment key still needs a rotation on my end before checkout is fully live. And the OS is early access, still hardening — the voice in particular is real but on a fallback engine while I tune a better one. No "it's all done" act.
