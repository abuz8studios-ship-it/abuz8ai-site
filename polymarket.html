How prediction markets work

🎯

What a prediction market is

A prediction market lets people trade shares in the outcome of a future event — "Will X happen?" Shares in the winning outcome resolve to $1; shares in losing outcomes resolve to $0. The trading price in between reflects what the crowd collectively believes.

📐

Price ≈ probability

Because a winning share pays exactly $1, a share trading at $0.41 implies the market assigns roughly a 41% chance to that outcome. That's why prediction markets are studied as live, money-weighted probability estimates — often sharper than polls.

🌊

Why prices move

News, evidence, and opinion shifts flow into buy and sell pressure. If traders think an outcome is underpriced they buy it, pushing the price — and the implied probability — up. Watching prices react to news is the fastest way to build probability intuition.

🧪

Why we built a paper tester

Reading about probability is one thing; feeling a position move against you is another. The paper tester gives you that feedback loop with zero risk: virtual dollars, real prices, honest P/L. Learn calibration before ever considering real markets — or instead of them entirely.

A worked example (real market, captured 2026-07-17)

"Will Argentina win the 2026 FIFA World Cup?" — "Yes" was trading at $0.4115, implying about a 41% chance. A $100 paper stake at $0.4115 buys ≈ 243 virtual shares. If the price later rises to $0.50, the position is worth ≈ $121.50 (+$21.50 paper P/L). If Argentina were eliminated, "Yes" would head toward $0 and the paper stake toward $0 too.

Using the tester

  1. Browse the live board

    Open the Live Markets tab. Markets are ranked by 24-hour volume — the crowd's current focus. Each price is the market-implied probability.

  2. Form your own estimate

    Before staking, decide what probability you would assign. The gap between your estimate and the market price is your (paper) edge — or your mistake.

  3. Open a paper position

    In the Paper Tester tab, pick the market, the outcome, and a virtual stake between $100 and $10,000 from your $1,000 starting bankroll.

  4. Track your P/L

    Positions are revalued at the current fetched price every time the page loads the feed. Green means the market moved toward your view.

  5. Close and review

    Close a position to bank the virtual result, then ask: was I right for the right reason? Reset any time to start the experiment over at $1,000.
