The discipline behind the badges

A roadmap is only as honest as the process that feeds it. This is ours — the same rules we apply internally, in public.

  1. Verify from disk, not from memory

    Nothing gets called "done" because someone remembers finishing it. A claim of LIVE requires the file on disk, the page returning 200, and the feature working in a real browser — re-checked, not recalled.

  2. Truth-Status on everything

    Every product, tool, and feature carries LIVE, EARLY ACCESS, or ROADMAP. The badge is a contract: we never sell what doesn't exist, and we never imply a download, model, or repo that isn't really there.

  3. Honest fallbacks, never fake numbers

    Live feeds (GitHub trending, agent stars, Hugging Face) can go offline. When they do, the page says so — a labeled cached snapshot or a plain "feed offline" note. Never an invented number, never a silently broken board.

  4. One change → test → next

    Ships are small and verified individually. Before overwriting anything we back it up; after writing anything we re-read it from disk. Boring, and it works.

  5. Public postmortems

    When we break something, we say so on /audit.html. Example: on 2026-07-17 our sitemap.xml shipped truncated to production. We found it, repaired it (422 URLs, parse-verified), and redeployed the same morning — and then wrote it down where you can read it.

Why no quarters? Date promises rot into fake claims the moment reality slips. We publish direction and current status instead, and we move items between lanes only when the work is verifiably done. "In progress" is a promise we can always keep; "Q3" often isn't.

Roadmap FAQ

Straight answers about how this roadmap works.

When will ABUZ8 OS be publicly available?
We don't promise dates or quarters. ABUZ8 OS is real and in active development (internal builds at v2.55.x), and access opens in early-access waves. Request access and we'll contact you when a wave opens.
Why are there no dates or quarters on this roadmap?
Because a date we can't keep is a fake claim. Our Truth-Status contract only lets us say three things about any item: LIVE (working today, verifiable), EARLY ACCESS (exists, gated), or ROADMAP (planned, not built). Items move between lanes when the work is actually done — not when a calendar says so.
Can I buy anything on the ROADMAP lane?
No. Nothing on the ROADMAP lane is for sale, ever. We never put a buy button on something that doesn't exist. The store only sells products that exist today, with instant download — every one tested before it's sold.
Does ABUZ8 publish models on Hugging Face today?
Not yet. The abuz8 Hugging Face account exists but has zero public models, datasets, or spaces today. Publishing sovereign models and datasets there is a ROADMAP item — planned, not built.
Where can I see what went wrong and how it was fixed?
On /audit.html. We audit our own site and publish what we find — for example, on 2026-07-17 our sitemap.xml shipped truncated to production; it was found, repaired (422 URLs, parse-verified), and redeployed the same morning.
