Common Questions

Does ABUZ8 branding appear anywhere on my platform? ▼

This program isn't live yet, but the design goal is zero ABUZ8 branding on any customer-facing surface — your logo, your domain, your colors, with an NDA available on request. Join the waitlist to be notified if it ships.

Would I keep 100% of customer revenue? ▼

That's the intended model: you'd set your own prices, bill customers through your own Stripe account, and keep everything, with ABUZ8 charging a flat license fee instead of a revenue share. This is a design goal, not a current offer — no license product exists to buy today.

What happens when new tools are added? ▼

The plan is for license holders to get new tools added to their bundle automatically at no extra cost as we ship them. This is a roadmap idea, not something you can sign up for today.

Would I be able to build my own frontend on top of an API? ▼

We're considering a tier with full REST API access for custom frontends or integrations. It isn't built or available yet — it's an idea we're gauging interest in through the waitlist.

What's the minimum commitment? ▼

We haven't finalized pricing or terms, because the program doesn't exist yet. The intent is month-to-month with no annual lock-in — treat that as a design goal, not a current offer.
