How it actually works

01

Route

Your prompt and constraints hit the brain router. It picks the workflow that fits — speed, fidelity, or audio — and queues the job.

02

Local render

ComfyUI on dual RTX 5090s. Source images and prompts never leave our infrastructure. No third-party API.

03

Motion lock

For I2V, motion direction is conditioned per frame using ControlNet to prevent the subject from drifting between keyframes.

04

Delivery

MP4 at 24fps, 1080p (or 1920x1080 / 1080x1920 depending on aspect). Direct download. Early-access members get priority queue.
