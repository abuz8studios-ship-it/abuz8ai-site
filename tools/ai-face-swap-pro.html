How it actually works

01

Identity extraction

ComfyUI detects the face region in the target photo and generates a precision inpainting mask so only the face is replaced.

03

Your selected mode adjusts age attributes, color grade, lighting, or style — applied during the inpainting diffusion pass for coherent integration.
