Cover Letter Best Practices

📌 Lead With Impact

Start with your biggest relevant achievement, not "I am applying for..." Hiring managers read dozens per day.

🎯 Match Their Language

Mirror keywords from the job description. ATS systems scan for matches. Our AI does this automatically.

📊 Quantify Everything

"Grew revenue 40%" beats "improved revenue." The more specific data you input, the stronger the output.

⚡ Keep It Under 400 Words

Hiring managers spend 7 seconds on average. Dense paragraphs lose. Crisp, confident prose wins.
