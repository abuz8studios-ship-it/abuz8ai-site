How it actually works

01

Prompt synthesis

Your text description is tokenized and enriched with category-specific acoustic priors — reverb profile, frequency range, and dynamic envelope matching your chosen SFX category.

02

AudioCraft diffusion

Meta's AudioCraft model runs a diffusion pass conditioned on your prompt and optional visual embeddings. The model synthesizes raw audio in the latent space before decoding.

03

Duration control

The decoded audio is trimmed or looped to match your requested duration. Pro quality mode applies a final mastering pass — EQ, transient shaping, and loudness normalization.

04

Local delivery

Renders on private RTX 5090 hardware in ~8 seconds. WAV delivered inline for immediate playback. Download in one click. Nothing stored after your session.
