How It Works

Four steps to cartoon.

No technical knowledge needed. Upload, pick a style, generate.

STEP 01

Upload your photo

Drop in a portrait, pet photo, landscape, or object. Or skip the photo and describe what you want in the text prompt.

STEP 02

Choose your style

Pick from 8 curated styles — Classic Cartoon, 3D CGI, Disney, Comic Book, Watercolor, Flat Vector, Chibi, or Retro 80s.

STEP 03

Adjust strength

Dial in how much the original photo shows through. Lower = subtle cartoon touches. Higher = full artistic transformation.

STEP 04

Download & use

ComfyUI renders on RTX 5090 in ~15 seconds. Download 1024×1024 PNG. No watermarks. Commercial use cleared.

FAQ

Questions answered.

Everything you need to know before joining early access.

Can I turn a photo of a real person into a cartoon? ▼

Yes. Upload a portrait and the model will redraw the person in your selected cartoon style while preserving likeness, expression, and pose. Works best with clear, well-lit front-facing photos.

What cartoon styles are available? ▼

8 curated styles: Classic Cartoon (bold outlines, vibrant fills), 3D CGI (Pixar/DreamWorks quality), Disney Animation (fluid expressive aesthetic), Comic Book (Marvel/DC panel art with halftones), Watercolor Illustration (soft painted look), Flat Vector (clean geometric shapes), Super Chibi (exaggerated cute proportions), and Retro Saturday Morning (80s cartoon nostalgia).

Does my photo get sent to third-party servers? ▼

No. All inference runs on local RTX 5090 hardware. Your photo is processed on our private infrastructure and deleted immediately after generation. When QADIR OS ships for local install, it runs entirely on your own machine.

Can I use cartoon outputs commercially? ▼

Early access outputs are cleared for personal and commercial use — client work, social content, merchandise designs, YouTube thumbnails, and brand mascots are all covered. Full commercial licensing terms will be published at launch.

Does it work for animals and objects, not just people? ▼

Yes. The model handles pets, cars, landscapes, food, products, and objects. Classic Cartoon is particularly effective on animals. Comic Book works well on vehicles and architecture. Watercolor excels on landscapes.

How fast is generation? ▼

10–25 seconds per image on RTX 5090. Classic Cartoon and Flat Vector render fastest. 3D CGI and Comic Book with complex shading take slightly longer. Queue wait depends on load.

Can I generate from a text prompt without a photo? ▼

Yes. Describe your cartoon scene ('a brave cartoon knight standing before a dragon castle at sunset') and the model generates it in your chosen style. Combining a photo and a prompt gives the strongest results.

What is the output resolution? ▼

Default output is 1024×1024 pixels. Portrait (1024×1536) and landscape (1536×1024) orientations will be supported in early access. All outputs are watermark-free.
