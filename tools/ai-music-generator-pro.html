Frequently Asked Questions

What AI model generates the music?

We use Meta's AudioCraft (MusicGen) — a state-of-the-art text-to-music model that generates coherent, high-quality music from natural language descriptions.

Is the generated music royalty-free?

Yes. All music generated through ABUZ8 tools is royalty-free and cleared for commercial use — YouTube, Spotify, ads, film, games, and any other project.

What genres can it generate?

Virtually any genre: ambient, lo-fi, cinematic, hip-hop, EDM, jazz, classical, rock, folk, world music, and more. The more specific your prompt, the better the result.

How long can the tracks be?

From 15 seconds to 5 minutes per generation. Longer tracks can be created by chaining generations together using our long-video stitcher pipeline.

Can I use this music for YouTube without Content ID claims?

Yes. AI-generated music through ABUZ8 is not registered in any Content ID system. You can monetize your YouTube videos freely.

What audio formats are available?

WAV (uncompressed, 44.1kHz 16-bit) and MP3 (320kbps). WAV for professional production; MP3 for social media and streaming.

How fast is generation?

Typically 5–15 seconds for a 30-second track on RTX 5090. A 3-minute track takes approximately 30–60 seconds to generate.

Does it run in the cloud or locally?

Locally on our RTX 5090 infrastructure during early access. When QADIR OS ships, you can run it on your own hardware with 8GB+ VRAM.
