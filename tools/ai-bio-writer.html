📌 LinkedIn Bio Best Practices

  • Lead with what you do, not your title
  • Include a quantifiable achievement in the first sentence
  • Third person for formal use; first person for LinkedIn
  • Keep it under 220 words for LinkedIn "About" section
  • End with a CTA: what you're looking for / open to

𝕏 Twitter Bio Tips

  • Max 160 characters — every word counts
  • Lead with your biggest hook or niche
  • Use emojis sparingly as separators
  • Include what you build or create

🎤 Speaker Bio Formula

  • Third person, 150-200 words
  • Credential hook → 3 key wins → topic expertise → CTA
  • Mention past speaking gigs or media features
