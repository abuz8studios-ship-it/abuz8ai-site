Common questions

Will the QR code still scan after being turned into art?
Yes. ControlNet QR Monster is specifically trained to preserve QR code structure while applying artistic styles. We test every output against multiple QR scanners before returning it. If scan confidence drops below threshold, we automatically lower the art strength and re-render.
What is ControlNet QR Monster?
QR Monster is a ControlNet model fine-tuned specifically on QR code structures. It uses the QR code geometry as a control signal while a diffusion model paints an artistic scene around it, preserving the code pattern in the visual noise.
What resolution is the output?
Default output is 768×768 pixels — crisp enough for print at 2×2 inches at 300dpi. Enterprise tier will support 2K output. Request it from early access if you need it sooner.
Can I upload my own style image?
Yes. Toggle "Upload a style reference image" in the workbench. Upload your brand assets, a painting, or any image — the model blends your visual identity with the QR structure.
What URLs can I use?
Any valid URL: websites, Instagram profiles, WhatsApp links, Stripe payment links, Google Maps locations, Linktree pages — anything that resolves. We do not scan the destination for content; the URL is purely structural data for the QR code.
Can I use this for commercial print?
Yes. The PNG output is print-ready for standard use (business cards, flyers, packaging). For large-format print (posters, signage, billboards), request a 2K export from early access — we can render it.
Do I need my own GPU?
No. Early access renders on our local RTX 5090 infrastructure. When QADIR OS ships, you can run it on your own hardware or use our cloud subscription.
How many can I generate?
Early access includes unlimited generation. We may apply fair-use limits if demand spikes. Priority rendering goes to early-access subscribers first.
