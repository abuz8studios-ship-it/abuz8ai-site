How it actually works

01

Subject isolation

The product is segmented from its original background and its structural latent — shape, edges, label, and proportions — is locked so the item itself stays recognizable through the relight pass.

02

Scene conditioning

Your chosen scene preset (or uploaded background reference) is encoded into a text and visual conditioning vector that defines the surface, backdrop, and studio lighting direction.

03

img2img relighting

ComfyUI runs an img2img studio pass at the chosen strength, rebuilding the environment, contact shadows, and reflections so the product sits convincingly in a real commercial set.

04

Local delivery

Renders on private RTX 5090 hardware in ~12 seconds. Full-res, watermark-free PNG delivered directly in the browser. Nothing stored after download.
