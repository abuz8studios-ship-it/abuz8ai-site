Common questions

How does the AI headshot generator work? +
Upload one clear selfie. Our consistent-character model (built on Stable Diffusion XL plus a custom identity LoRA) locks your face from that one reference, then renders 50 studio-grade variants across your chosen style, outfit, and background. Each variant keeps your face recognizable while changing pose, lighting, wardrobe, and environment. Pipeline: comfyui_consistent_character → comfyui_studio → high-res upscale.
Will it actually look like me? +
Yes — that’s the entire point. We use a consistent-character pipeline that locks your face identity from one reference photo and varies only the wardrobe, background, lighting, and pose. People who know you recognize you immediately. Output is professional and aspirational, not synthetic. If a particular variant drifts, we surface it for your review and you skip it from the pack.

Twelve styles: Corporate Studio, Executive Luxury, Modern Office, Tech Founder, Creative Startup, Diplomatic Formal, Cinematic Editorial, Medical / Lab Coat, Academic / Legal Robe, Outdoor Natural, Press / Journalism, and Bold Brand Color. Each has a tuned prompt template and (during Early Access) a dedicated LoRA adapter for production quality.
