Frequently Asked Questions

What is consistent character AI?

Consistent character AI generates multiple images of the same character while keeping facial features, proportions, and style identical — solving the classic problem where AI faces change from image to image.

How consistent is the face across scenes?

Very. Our engine uses IP-Adapter Plus and ControlNet facial conditioning. The same eyes, nose structure, and facial proportions appear across all your scenes — including different angles and expressions.

What art styles are supported?

Photorealistic, anime, manga, 3D CGI, oil painting, watercolor, flat vector, comic book, fantasy illustration, and more. Consistency is maintained across style switches.

Can I use a real photo as the character reference?

Yes. Upload a photo (with consent) and the AI will generate consistent scenes featuring that likeness in different styles and settings.

Is this useful for comics and webtoons?

Absolutely. Generate your main characters once, then produce an entire comic chapter with the same cast — consistent faces, panel after panel.

How many scenes can I generate from one reference?

Unlimited. The reference image is saved and reused for every new scene you request. One character, infinite content.

Can I use these characters commercially?

Yes. All images generated through ABUZ8 tools are yours to use for commercial projects, publications, and products.

What model powers this?

ComfyUI with IP-Adapter Plus and ControlNet facial conditioning, running on local RTX 5090. No character data is sent to third-party APIs.
