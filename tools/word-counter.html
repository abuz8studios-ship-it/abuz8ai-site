Frequently Asked Questions

Is this word counter free and private?
Yes. It is 100% free with no signup, and every calculation runs in your browser. Your text is never uploaded or stored on any server.
What is a good Flesch Reading Ease score?
A score of 60–70 is plain English that most adults read comfortably. Higher is easier; below 50 is fairly difficult and below 30 reads at a university-graduate level.
How is reading time calculated?
Reading time assumes about 200 words per minute (average silent reading); speaking time assumes about 130 words per minute for natural speech.
What is keyword density and why does it matter?
Keyword density is how often a word appears as a share of total words. It helps writers and SEOs confirm a target term is used enough to be relevant — without over-stuffing.
