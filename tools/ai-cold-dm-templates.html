💡 Cold DM Best Practices
→First line is everything. 80% of recipients read only the opener. Make it about them, not you.
→One ask per message. Ask for one specific, low-friction action — a call, a reply, a click.
→Personalize the opener. Reference a post, achievement, or mutual connection for 3× reply rate.
→Short wins. Under 150 words for DMs. Under 100 for Twitter/X.
→Follow up once. 50% of replies come from a single follow-up sent 3–5 days later.
