How it actually works

01

Content extraction

The content image is encoded by a VAE to extract its structural latent representation — composition, edges, depth, and spatial layout — preserved through the style pass.

02

Style embedding

Your chosen style preset (or uploaded style reference) is encoded into a text and/or visual conditioning vector that guides the diffusion model's aesthetic output at every denoising step.

03

img2img conditioning

ComfyUI runs a noised img2img diffusion pass at the chosen strength. The model reconstructs the image structure while re-rendering all surface detail in the target art style.

04

Local delivery

Renders on private RTX 5090 hardware in ~10 seconds. Full-res PNG delivered directly in the browser. Nothing stored after download.
