Process

How It Works

Four steps from your photo to gallery-ready anime art.

STEP 01

Upload or Describe

Drop in a photo (portrait, landscape, or object) or type what you want the scene to look like. Photo + prompt together gives the strongest results.

STEP 02

Choose Your Style

Pick from 8 curated anime aesthetics — from Studio Ghibli's painterly warmth to Cyberpunk Anime's electric urban grit. Each style is a fine-tuned preset, not a keyword guess.

STEP 03

Set the Intensity

Dial in how much anime transformation you want. Subtle overlay at 0.5, or full stylistic rebirth at 0.95. The slider gives you exact control.

STEP 04

Download & Use

ComfyUI renders on a local RTX 5090 in 10–25 seconds. Download 1024×1024 PNG with zero watermarks. Use commercially. No attribution required.

Questions

Frequently Asked

Everything you need to know before generating.

Can I use a photo of a real person? ▾

Yes. Upload a portrait and the model will redraw the person in your selected anime style while preserving facial structure, expression, and pose. Works best with clear, front-facing photos under good lighting.

What is the output resolution? ▾

Default output is 1024×1024 pixels. Portrait orientation (1024×1536) and landscape (1536×1024) will be supported in early access. All outputs are watermark-free at full resolution.

Does my photo get sent to third-party servers? ▾

No. Inference runs on local RTX 5090 hardware at ABUZ8. Your photo is processed on private infrastructure and deleted immediately after generation. When QADIR OS ships for local install, everything runs on your own machine — your photos never leave your device.

Which anime styles are available? ▾

8 curated styles: Studio Ghibli (painterly, nature-lit), Shonen Manga (bold linework, high energy), Cyberpunk Anime (neon-lit cityscapes), Chibi (cute, big-head proportions), Manhwa K-Drama (Korean webtoon), Vintage 80s Anime (film grain, cel-shaded), Dark Fantasy (atmospheric, high-contrast), and Slice of Life (warm, everyday scenes).

Can I use outputs commercially? ▾

Yes. Early access outputs are for personal and commercial use. Use them for client work, merchandise, book covers, game assets, or social media content. Full commercial licensing terms will be published at launch. No attribution required.

Does it work for landscapes and objects, not just people? ▾

Yes. The model handles portraits, landscapes, cityscapes, animals, architecture, and objects. Studio Ghibli style particularly excels on natural scenes and backgrounds. Cyberpunk and Dark Fantasy work well on architectural and urban subjects.

How fast is generation? ▾

10–25 seconds per image on RTX 5090 at 30 steps. Generation time depends on style complexity and selected step count. Ghibli and Slice of Life render fastest; Dark Fantasy and Cyberpunk with complex lighting take slightly longer. Higher step count = better quality, longer wait.

Can I generate from a text prompt without a photo? ▾

Yes. The text prompt field is fully supported — describe your scene and the model generates it in your chosen anime style. Photo + prompt combined gives the strongest results (photo provides structure, prompt provides creative direction), but prompt-only works well for original scenes.
