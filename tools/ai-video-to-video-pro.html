How It Works

Four steps to a new look.

Upload your footage, configure, transform. No technical knowledge required.

STEP 01

Upload your clip

Drop in any MP4, MOV, or WEBM file. Clips up to 60 seconds. 200MB max. 1080p or lower for best results.

STEP 02

Choose a style

Pick from 8 curated visual styles — Cinematic, Anime, Oil Painting, Watercolor, Cyberpunk, Cartoon, Ghibli, or Film Noir.

STEP 03

Set strength & quality

Control how heavily the style is applied. Lower values keep the original footage grounded; higher values go full transformation.

Download your video

FAQ

Questions answered.

Everything you need to know before joining early access.

What video formats are supported? ▼

MP4 (H.264/H.265), MOV, and WEBM. Maximum upload size is 200MB. For best results, use clips under 30 seconds and 1080p or lower resolution.

How long does processing take? ▼

A 10-second 720p clip renders in approximately 2–5 minutes on RTX 5090. Cinematic Film and Oil Painting are the most computationally intensive styles. Cartoon and Watercolor are fastest.

Does the output stay temporally consistent? ▼

Yes. The VACE engine processes frames with temporal coherence — the style applies consistently across frames without flickering or drift. This is the key advantage over simple frame-by-frame style transfer.

Is my video uploaded to third-party servers? ▼

No. Your video is processed entirely on our local RTX 5090 hardware and deleted immediately after processing. No third-party AI services handle your content.

What is the maximum clip length? ▼

Early access supports clips up to 60 seconds. For longer content, split your video into segments and use the AI Long Video Stitcher to combine the styled clips into a seamless final edit.

Can I use outputs commercially? ▼

Yes. Outputs are cleared for personal and commercial use — client videos, YouTube content, brand reels, and advertising are all covered.
