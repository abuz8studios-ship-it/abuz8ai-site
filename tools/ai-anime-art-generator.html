Common questions

What anime styles does the generator support? +
12 styles: Modern Anime, Classic 90s, Ghibli-Inspired, Shonen Action, Shojo Romance, Seinen Realistic, Manhwa Webtoon, Chibi/SD, Cyberpunk Anime, Dark Fantasy, Slice of Life, and Oil-Painting Anime. Each style has a tuned prompt template and (during Early Access) a dedicated LoRA adapter.
Is the output royalty-free? +
Yes. Every generation is royalty-free for commercial and personal use. You own the output. Sell prints, publish webcomics, license to studios — no attribution required, no revenue share, no take-backs.
How long does each render take? +
Single illustrations: 8–25 seconds on the live RTX 5090 backend. Character sheets (4 angles): 30–60 seconds. Full 6-panel manga pages: 60–90 seconds. Render times scale linearly with detail level and resolution.
Can I generate the same character across different scenes? +
Yes — that's exactly what Character Sheet mode is for. Generate a 4-angle reference, then attach it as a control image for unlimited follow-up scenes. Same face, same outfit, different lighting, different angle. Perfect for webcomics and games.
Does it copy real anime characters? +
No. We deliberately exclude character names from prompts and the model is tuned to generate original characters in established art styles. We won't reproduce Naruto, Goku, Spy×Family, etc. Style is fair use; specific characters are not.
What if the live GPU is offline? +
If the RTX 5090 backend is offline (status pill at bottom-right shows red), your prompt is queued to your Early Access account. We render in batch when the GPU comes back and email you the results within 30 minutes.
What resolution do I get? +
Default is 1024×1024 for square, scaled proportionally for other aspect ratios. Early Access tier unlocks 2048×2048 ultra-detail mode. Manga panel pages render at 2480×3508 (300dpi A4-equivalent) — actually print-ready.
