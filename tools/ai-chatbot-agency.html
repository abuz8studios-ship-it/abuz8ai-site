From Zero to Live in 72 Hours

Here's what a white-glove deployment could look like — you provide docs, we handle the rest.

📋
Hour 0 · Kickoff
Discovery & Knowledge Intake
You share your docs, FAQs, product data, and tone guide. We ingest everything into Zait's RAG knowledge base.
🧠
Hours 4–24 · Training
Bot Training & Personality Setup
Zait trains on your knowledge base. We configure intents, fallbacks, escalation triggers, and brand voice.
🔗
Hours 24–48 · Integration
CRM & Tool Integrations
We wire up HubSpot, Salesforce, Zendesk, Calendly — whichever tools you use. Every lead and ticket syncs automatically.
🧪
Hours 48–68 · QA
Testing & Edge Case Coverage
100-message stress test. Edge case training. You review and approve before anything goes live.
🚀
Hour 72 · Live
Deploy & Monitor
One script tag on your site. The bot goes live. We monitor performance for the first 30 days and retrain based on real conversations.
