Our security posture, in plain language

abuz8ai.com is a static website hosted on Cloudflare Pages, with a small number of Pages Functions behind /api/*. There are no user accounts, no passwords, and no databases of personal information beyond a single waitlist table. The less we collect, the less can go wrong — that is the core of our security model.

📄

Static-first

Pages are pre-built HTML served from Cloudflare's edge. No server-side sessions, no login surface to attack.

📉

Minimal data

The only thing a visitor can submit is the waitlist form: an email, a product interest, and the source page. Nothing else is collected.

💳

Stripe-handled payments

Checkout happens on stripe.com under Stripe's PCI-DSS compliance. Card data never touches our servers.

🔒

TLS everywhere

HTTPS is enforced by Cloudflare on every page and every API route.

Honest scope: we are a small company. We do not hold SOC 2 or ISO 27001 certifications, and we do not claim enterprise compliance programs we don't run. What we do instead is keep the attack surface tiny and tell you exactly what exists — see the Compliance tab above for the full status table.

Exactly what we collect

One endpoint on this site collects data: POST /api/waitlist. When you join a waitlist, we store your email address, the product you're interested in, and the page you submitted from — in a Cloudflare D1 database (abuz8_waitlist). That is the complete list.

  • Waitlist form: email + product interest + source page → Cloudflare D1. Used to tell you when the thing you asked about is ready.
  • No accounts, no passwords. You cannot create a login on this site, so there are no credentials for us to lose.
  • No tracking cookies from us. The site itself sets no tracking cookies.
  • No payment data on our servers. Card details are entered on stripe.com, never here.

Want your waitlist email deleted? Send a request to support@abuz8ai.com and it will be removed. We do not process personal data beyond the waitlist, so that one request covers everything we hold about you. Full details: Privacy Policy.

How payment security works here

Every purchase on the store uses a Stripe-hosted checkout link. When you click Buy, you leave our site and enter your card details on stripe.com.

  1. You click Buy on abuz8ai.com

    The button is a link to a Stripe-hosted checkout page. No payment form ever renders on our domain.

  2. Stripe handles the card

    Card entry, validation, and processing happen on stripe.com under Stripe's PCI-DSS compliance program.

  3. We never see card data

    Card numbers are never seen or stored by ABUZ8 — not in transit, not at rest. There is nothing card-related on our servers to breach.

Our promise: every store product is tested before it's sold, and we'll always be honest with you. If a product is broken, misdescribed, or your download can't be accessed, see Refunds for how we make it right.

Third-party note: Stripe may set its own cookies during checkout under its own policies — that happens on stripe.com, not here.

Where the site runs

abuz8ai.com is hosted on Cloudflare Pages — static pages served from Cloudflare's edge network, plus Pages Functions behind /api/* (public read-only feeds and the waitlist form). TLS/HTTPS is enforced by Cloudflare on all traffic.

Security headers served on every page

These three headers come from the site's _headers file, verbatim:

HeaderValueWhat it protects against
X-Content-Type-OptionsnosniffStops browsers from MIME-sniffing responses into executable types.
X-Frame-OptionsSAMEORIGINBlocks other sites from embedding our pages in iframes (clickjacking).
Referrer-Policystrict-origin-when-cross-originLimits what referrer information leaks to other sites you navigate to.

API surface — the routes that matter for your data

  • GET /api/github-trending — read-only feed of trending GitHub repos, parsed server-side. Collects nothing.
  • GET /api/github-agents — read-only star counts for 20 curated open-source agent projects. Collects nothing.
  • GET /api/polymarket — read-only proxy for public Polymarket market data (powers the paper-trading tester; no real money, no wallet connection). Collects nothing.
  • POST /api/waitlist — the only endpoint that stores data (email + product interest + source → Cloudflare D1).

Found a vulnerability? Tell us.

If you find a security issue anywhere on abuz8ai.com or its /api/* functions, we genuinely want to hear about it.

  1. Email the details

    Send a description to support@abuz8ai.com. Include the affected URL or endpoint, steps to reproduce, and what you observed. Screenshots or a proof-of-concept help.

  2. We acknowledge within 72 hours

    Our target is to acknowledge every report within 72 hours of receiving it.

  3. We fix and follow up

    We investigate, fix what's real, and let you know the outcome.

Honest note on bounties: there is no bug bounty program yet. We're a small company and won't pretend otherwise. What we can offer today is a fast acknowledgement, a real fix, and credit if you want it.

Please report privately by email first rather than publishing an issue publicly, so there's a chance to fix it before details circulate.

Common questions

Short, honest answers about how this site handles your data and your money.

Do you ever see my credit card number?
No. Checkout happens on stripe.com via Stripe-hosted checkout links. Card entry and processing are handled under Stripe's PCI-DSS compliance; card data never touches ABUZ8 servers.
What personal data do you collect?
Only what you submit to the waitlist form: your email, the product you're interested in, and the page you submitted from — stored in Cloudflare D1. There are no accounts, no passwords, and no tracking cookies set by this site.
How do I delete my data?
Email support@abuz8ai.com and your waitlist entry will be removed. Since the waitlist is the only personal data we hold, that single request covers everything.
Do you run ads or ad tracking?
A Google AdSense script placeholder exists on some pages, but no ads are currently served — there is no active publisher ID. The site itself sets no tracking cookies.
Are you SOC 2 or ISO 27001 certified?
No. We're a small company and we won't claim certifications we don't hold. Our actual posture: a static site, one data-collecting endpoint, Stripe-handled payments, TLS enforced by Cloudflare, and three security headers on every page.
Is there a bug bounty?
Not yet — honestly. Report vulnerabilities to support@abuz8ai.com; our target is acknowledgement within 72 hours, plus a real fix and credit if you want it.
