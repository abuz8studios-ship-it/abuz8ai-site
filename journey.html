THE HONEST VERSION

On March 25, 2026, this became a public project. ABUZ8 LLC is a one-person company in Ohio: founder Ahmad Abu Zait, working side by side with an AI partner, building on a real RTX 5090 rig that sits in the room — not a rented cloud story. That machine is where the models run, where the tools get tested, and where everything on this site was made.

The mission is simple to say and hard to do: sovereign local AI for everyone — models you run on your own hardware, with no cloud lock-in. The long-term case for that lives on the Sovereign AI page. This page is about the road there.

What building in public means here: the numbers on this page are counted from the actual files on disk before they are published. When something breaks, it gets written up on the audit page instead of quietly patched. When something isn't built yet, it is labeled ROADMAP and never sold. There are no revenue claims, no user counts, and no testimonials on this site — because none can be honestly made yet, and we would rather say that plainly than manufacture them.

So far the output is concrete: 180 free tools, 527 blog posts, a store with 1 execution-verified product (27 others removed 2026-07-30 after an honest-delivery audit) with Stripe-hosted checkout, and an agentic OS (ABUZ8 OS) in active internal development at v2.55. The next chapter is the hard one — turning real work into the first real customers. That goal is written on the Goals tab of this page, in public, so you can hold us to it.

"No fluff. No fake promises. Real tools that work." — that's the whole strategy.

REAL MILESTONES ONLY — EACH ONE VERIFIABLE ON THIS SITE

  1. 2026-03-25 Day 0 — the public start line

    A solo founder and an AI partner commit to building sovereign AI in public from Ohio, on a real RTX 5090 rig. Everything after this date happens in the open.

  2. Site + store go live on Cloudflare LIVE

    abuz8ai.com ships on Cloudflare Pages with serverless functions for the API routes and Stripe-hosted checkout for the store. Card data never touches our servers.

  3. 180 free tool pages shipped LIVE

    Browser dev tools (JSON formatter, regex tester, color and SEO utilities, and more) that run 100% client-side, plus AI generation tools that honestly degrade to a waitlist form when the local GPU backend is offline — no fake output, ever. Browse them at /tools.

  4. 527 blog posts published LIVE

    Guides and long-form posts at /blog/ — the written trail of what was learned while building.

  5. Store with 1 execution-verified product LIVE

    Instant-download digital product, tested end to end before it's sold. 27 other listings were removed 2026-07-30 after an honest-delivery audit found their downloads didn't match their pages — see the audit page. See the store.

  6. ABUZ8 OS internal builds reach v2.55 EARLY ACCESS

    The local-first Electron agentic OS is real and in active development — 800+ internal API routes and a self-hosted update channel. There is no public download yet; interest goes through /request.html. We will not fabricate a download link.

  7. 2026-07-17 Unified design system + 8 new hub pages — and a published failure

    The whole site moves to one design system and eight new hub pages ship. The same morning, our sitemap.xml shipped truncated to production — we found it in our own audit, repaired it (422 URLs, parse-verified), redeployed within the morning, and disclosed it on the audit page. That's the build-in-public deal: failures get published too.

Every count above is verified against the files on disk before it appears on this page. If a number here ever disagrees with what you can click, tell us: contact.

THE RULES WE BUILD BY — IN PUBLIC, SO THEY'RE ENFORCEABLE

🚫

No fake claims

No invented users, testimonials, revenue figures, "as seen in" logos, or fake urgency counters — ever. If a number can't be verified, it doesn't go on the site.

💾

Verify from disk

Claims are checked against the actual files and live pages before publishing — the tool count, the post count, the product count. Memory and wishful thinking don't count as evidence.

🚢

Ship real things

Nothing is sold that doesn't exist. Products in the store are real files you download the moment you buy. Unbuilt ideas are labeled ROADMAP and never carry a buy button.

📢

Publish failures

When we break something — like the truncated sitemap we shipped and repaired on 2026-07-17 — we disclose it instead of burying it. The audit page is the public record.

Honest questions, honest answers

The questions a skeptic should ask a build-in-public project — answered the way we'd want them answered.

Is ABUZ8 making money?
We do not publish revenue figures, user counts, or testimonials — and we will not until there are real, verifiable ones. Nothing on this site is an invented number. The current near-term goal is exactly what the Goals tab says: first customers for the products that already exist.
Can I download ABUZ8 OS?
Not yet. ABUZ8 OS is real and in active development (internal builds at v2.55), but there is no public download. It's early access by request at /request.html — and we will never fabricate a download link.
Are ABUZ8 models published on Hugging Face or GitHub?
No. The abuz8 Hugging Face account exists but has zero public models, datasets, or spaces today, and there is no public ABUZ8 GitHub organization. Publishing sovereign models and open-sourcing components are both ROADMAP items — planned, not shipped. See the Hugging Face hub and GitHub hub.
Who is building ABUZ8?
A solo founder — Ahmad Abu Zait of ABUZ8 LLC, Ohio — working with an AI partner, building and testing on a real RTX 5090 rig. Everything is built local-first and in public since 2026-03-25. The mission: sovereign local AI for everyone.
What data does this page collect?
Only what you submit to the waitlist form: your email, product interest, and the source page — stored in Cloudflare D1 and deleted on request via ahmad@abuz8ai.com. No accounts, no tracking cookies set by us, and no payment data ever touches our servers. Full detail: privacy.
