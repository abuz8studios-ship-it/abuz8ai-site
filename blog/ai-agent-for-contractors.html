AI Agent for Contractors: Kill the 9pm Paperwork Shift

NICHE PLAYBOOKSJULY 23, 20265 MIN READ

An AI agent for contractors is aimed at the shift nobody bids on: the one at the kitchen table at 9pm, writing quotes, chasing invoices, and answering emails after ten hours on site. Plumbers, electricians, remodelers, roofers — the work that pays happens with tools in hand, and the work that gets you paid happens in a folding chair with a laptop. The second shift is administrative, repetitive, and formatted the same way every time. Which makes it exactly the kind of work a machine should be doing.

No robot is sweating a pipe or pulling wire. This is about the office half of a trades business — what an agent can take tonight, free, and what's coming next.

Quotes: the job you do before the job

Every estimate is judgment (site conditions, materials, how much you actually want this job) wrapped in arithmetic and formatting. Keep the judgment; hand off the wrapper. The free AI pricing calculator helps you structure cost, margin, and price so you stop quoting off gut math at the end of a long day — the classic way trades underprice themselves. Write your numbers once as rules ("labor rate, materials plus 20, minimum call-out"), and let the tool grind the arithmetic every time.

Invoices: send them the day the job closes

The single most profitable habit in the trades is boring: invoice same-day, every day. Money you haven't billed is money you're lending interest-free. The AI invoice generator turns line items into a clean, professional PDF invoice in your browser, free, in about two minutes — from the truck, before you pull out of the driveway. Same-day invoicing plus a polite day-14 nudge beats any collections trick ever invented.

Look like the company you already are

Homeowners comparing three bids can't see your workmanship yet — they see your paperwork. A real logo on the quote, the invoice, and the yard sign closes the gap between "guy with a truck" and "company I trust with a deposit." The AI logo generator gets you a clean mark free, and the email signature generator makes every email you send carry the brand. Small levers, real conversion.
