ABUZ8 Just Shipped: A Unified Redesign and 8 New Hubs in One Day

BUILD IN PUBLICJULY 17, 20264 MIN READ

This morning abuz8ai.com looked like three different websites stitched together: a dark homepage, light-themed newer pages, and a few hundred tool and blog pages somewhere in between. By 8:22 AM EDT it was one site — one palette (Islamic green, lapis blue, gold), one design system, and eight entirely new hub pages. This post is the honest changelog.

The 8 new hubs

Sovereign AI — the mission page. Why we believe AI you own — models running on your own hardware, your data on your own disk — matters, and how to start today with hardware tiers from an 8GB GPU up.

Security & Compliance — exactly how this site handles your data, including what we are not. One data-collecting endpoint, Stripe-hosted payments we never see, and an honest "no SOC2 certificate" line, because pretending otherwise is how trust dies.

Audit & Transparency — our Truth-Status contract (LIVE / EARLY ACCESS / ROADMAP) and a public incident log. Today's entry: our own sitemap shipped corrupted to production, we found it, repaired it, and redeployed the same morning.

Roadmap — what works today, what's gated behind early access, and what is planned-but-not-built. Nothing on the roadmap has a buy button. That's the point.

The Journey & Goals — building in public from Day 0 (March 25, 2026): real milestones, real goals, zero revenue theater.

Hugging Face Hub — live trending models, datasets, and spaces from the public Hugging Face API, plus a deep run-models-locally guide: GGUF, quantization levels, and what actually fits in your VRAM.

GitHub Hub — weekly and monthly trending boards plus live star charts for 20 open-source agent frameworks, all credited and linked to their authors.

Polymarket Paper Tester — live prediction-market data plus a paper-trading simulator with a virtual bankroll stored only in your browser. No real money, no wallets, not financial advice — a safe way to learn how market prices map to probabilities.

What else changed

The products page went from a redirect stub to a real 28-product catalog. The news hub and agents hub were rebuilt with tabbed live boards. The store joined the unified dark theme with every checkout link verified byte-identical before and after. And a new on-page guide — the gold button in the corner — answers questions about the site, the tools, and deploying your own agents, entirely in your browser.

Less visible but more important: a full static QA pass over all 180 free tools found 47 pages with real defects — corrupted file tails, scripts loading from wrong paths, and JavaScript that silently died in browsers. All fixed, all verified, all live. If a tool page failed for you before today, it's worth a second look.

Why we publish this

Most sites announce redesigns with adjectives. We'd rather show the incident log next to the feature list. The whole premise of ABUZ8 is that you can verify what we claim: the tools run in your browser, the feeds credit their sources, and the audit page documents what broke. That's the standard we want to be held to.

Start anywhere: 180 free tools · the store · the mission. No signup, no tracking, no fake claims.
