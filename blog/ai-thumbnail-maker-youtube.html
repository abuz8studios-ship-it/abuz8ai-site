How to make a thumbnail that earns the click

Design for the phone, not the desktop

Most views happen on a small screen where your thumbnail is barely bigger than a postage stamp. If the text isn't readable at that size, it isn't readable. Use three or four big words, not a sentence. Test by shrinking it down before you publish.

One subject, high contrast, clear emotion

The thumbnails that win usually have a single clear focal point — a face with a real expression, or one bold object — against a contrasting background. Clutter loses. A surprised face beats a tidy logo almost every time.

Make the thumbnail and title a team, not a duplicate

Don't put your title text on the thumbnail word-for-word. The title says one thing; the thumbnail adds a second. Together they create the curiosity gap that earns the click. Repeating yourself wastes half the space.
