AI Agent for Event Planners: Run the Chaos, Not the Paperwork

NICHE PLAYBOOKSJULY 23, 20265 MIN READ

An AI agent for event planners targets the paradox of the job: you're paid to be calm inside chaos, but the thing burning you out isn't the chaos — it's the paperwork orbiting it. Proposals for clients who might book, recap notes from six vendor calls, invoices in three payment stages, signage files, seating logistics, the follow-up email you compose in your head at 1am. The event is the craft. The document storm around it is clerical gravity, and clerical gravity is what agents are built to cancel.

Here's the honest division of labor — what AI actually takes off a planner's plate today with free tools, and what a full agent loop adds tomorrow.

Proposals: your taste, minus the formatting hours

A proposal is a sales document wearing a design dress — concept, run-of-show, budget tiers, terms. The thinking is yours; the deck-building is data entry. The free AI presentation maker turns your outline into structured slides in the browser, so a "can you send something by Friday?" stops costing you a Thursday night. Win or lose the pitch on your concept, not on whether you had time to make it look finished.

Vendor calls: if it isn't written down, it didn't happen

Every planner has lost a deposit-sized dispute to "but you said." Caterer call, florist call, venue walkthrough — the AI meeting notes tool structures your raw notes into decisions, action items, and owners you can forward as a recap the same hour. That recap email is quiet professional armor: it prevents the misunderstanding and documents it if it happens anyway.

Check-in that doesn't need a folding table

The AI QR art generator makes codes that belong on a designed event: welcome signage that links to schedules and menus, table cards that open the playlist request, check-in codes for registration. QR codes were always ugly; they don't have to be. Guests get answers off your radio, and you get fifty fewer "where's the bathroom schedule" interruptions per event.

Money: three-stage billing without spreadsheet archaeology

Deposit, progress payment, final balance — event billing has structure, which means it can be templated. The AI invoice generator produces clean staged invoices free in the browser. Send the final invoice the morning after the event, while the client still has confetti in their inbox — the same-day-invoice rule prints money in every service business, and events are no exception.

Destination events: the logistics draft

For out-of-town programs, the AI travel planner drafts day-by-day logistics skeletons — arrivals, activity blocks, buffers — that you refine with the local knowledge only you have. First drafts in minutes mean your expertise gets spent on judgment, not on typing "Day 1:" again.
