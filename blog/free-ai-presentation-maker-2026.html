The problem with building slides the old way

Traditional slide-building is a time trap disguised as productive work. You spend four hours arranging boxes, choosing between two nearly identical shades of blue, and pasting the same company logo into 14 slide masters. Then you realize your deck doesn't have an argument. It has decorations.

Here's what actually kills most presentations:

  • You start from layout, not from argument. Picking a template before you know what the deck is saying is like choosing a font before you've written the sentence.
  • Every slide tries to do everything. Seven bullet points, two charts, a pull quote, and an icon. The audience retains none of it because cognitive load just hit the ceiling.
  • There's no through-line. Each slide stands alone instead of advancing the audience from where they are to the decision you need.
  • Speaker notes don't exist. So every slide is both the visual aid AND the script, which produces walls of text nobody reads.

A deck built this way takes 4-8 hours and still gets the "I'm not sure what the ask is" response in the room. That's the problem the tool solves.
