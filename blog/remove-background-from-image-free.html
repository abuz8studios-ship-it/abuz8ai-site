If you want to remove the background from an image free, the usual trade is hidden in the fine print: the tool is free, but your photo gets uploaded to someone's server, processed there, and kept under retention terms you never read. Our background remover studio makes the opposite trade. The page is free, there is no signup and no watermark — and the cutout is computed on your own machine, because the page drives an image engine running locally on your hardware. The photo never travels.

How a browser page can process an image without uploading it

The studio page is a control panel, not a processing farm. When you open it, it looks for a ComfyUI engine running on your own computer at its standard local address. If it finds one, the Generate button goes live: your browser hands the image to the engine over your own loopback connection, the engine runs a dedicated background-removal model, and the finished PNG with transparency comes back to your screen. Nothing in that round trip crosses the internet. If no engine is found, the page says so plainly and shows the early-access path instead — it will not pretend to work.

What actually does the cutting

Under the hood the workflow uses the RemBG family of segmentation models — the same isnet-general-use model that professional pipelines use for product shots and portraits. It was wired and tested against a live engine before the page shipped: the first run takes longer while the model loads, and after that a cutout comes back in a few seconds on a decent GPU. This is not a fuzzy "AI eraser" that smears edges. Segmentation models trace hair, fur, and product edges properly, and you get a real alpha channel out, not a white fill.

Why "no upload" is the whole point: product photos leak launches, ID photos leak faces, and client work leaks clients. A background remover you run locally is the difference between a tool and a disclosure. The render happens on your GPU; the only thing our site does is serve the page and count the visit.

What you need to run it

Two things: a computer with a reasonable GPU, and ComfyUI — the free, open-source image engine — installed and running. The studio page detects it automatically and tells you exactly what is missing if a model is not installed. Frequently asked questions

Is this really free, or free-until-a-paywall? Really free. There is no account system, no credit counter, and no watermark to remove. The economics work because your GPU does the rendering — we serve a static page and never touch the image, so there is no per-render cost for anyone to recover.
