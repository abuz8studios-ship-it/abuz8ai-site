Black Friday AI Marketing Checklist — What to Finish by October

ECOMMERCEAUGUST 19, 20265 MIN READ

Black Friday isn't won on Black Friday. It's won in the six weeks before, and lost in the last-minute scramble. This Black Friday AI marketing checklist is ordered by deadline, not by importance — because in Q4 the deadline is the importance. Everything below is buildable by one person with AI tools; the only thing AI can't generate is the head start, which is why you're reading this in August and not November.

By mid-September: decide the offer

Everything downstream depends on one sentence: what's the deal, on what, for whom, for how long. Sitewide percentage, bundle, gift-with-purchase, doorbuster — pick it now, because the creative, the email sequence, and the ad copy all encode it. AI accelerates production, but it can't make this decision for you, and changing it in November means regenerating everything.

By end of September: the creative library

Build your deal creative with the AI product photo generator — hero images per product in gifting scenes, plus square, vertical and wide crops for every placement you run. Add short video: a 6–15 second cut from the AI video generator per hero product, because video placements are where Q4 attention actually lives. The full photo strategy is in our holiday product photos guide, and the video angle in AI holiday video ads.

By mid-October: copy, email, and landing pages

Draft ad copy variants with the ad copy generator — at least five hooks per product, because Black Friday auctions burn creative fast and you'll want replacements queued. Write the email sequence now: teaser, early access, launch, last call. Test every subject line through the email subject tester — inboxes on Black Friday weekend are a war zone and the subject line is most of the battle. The sequence structure is covered in AI holiday email campaigns. If your deal spans multiple products, build a gift guide page — the AI gift guide workflow turns your catalog into one in an afternoon.

Five hooks per product is the floor. Q4 creative fatigues in days, not weeks. The sellers who keep winning auctions in late November are the ones swapping in pre-built variants while everyone else is generating in a panic.

By early November: test and schedule

Run small-budget tests on your ad variants in early November while CPMs are still sane — the data tells you which creative deserves the real budget on the weekend itself. Schedule the email sequence. Load your social calendar (the Q4 content calendar guide covers cadence). Then the week itself is monitoring and variant-swapping, not production.

The meta-point: AI moves the bottleneck

Before AI tools, the bottleneck was production — you simply couldn't afford fifty creative variants. Now production is nearly free and the bottleneck is decision and deadline discipline. Frequently asked questions

When do Black Friday CPMs start rising? Meaningfully from mid-October, sharply in November. Creative tested in early November costs a fraction of the same test two weeks later.

Is Black Friday worth it for tiny stores? Yes, but narrow: one clear offer, your existing email list, and organic social beat a broad underfunded ad push.

How many emails is too many? Four over the week is standard; on the weekend itself, daily is expected and unsubscribes are the cost of doing business.
