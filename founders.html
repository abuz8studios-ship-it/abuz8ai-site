The Problem Nobody Says Out Loud

You don't own your AI. You rent your own mind back.

Every mainstream AI today is a meter running in someone else's building. Your prompts are logged on servers you'll never see. Your monthly bill never ends. The model you depend on can be deprecated, throttled, or re-priced overnight — and you have no say.

And the cost isn't only yours. Cloud AI runs on datacenters that consume staggering amounts of electricity and fresh water, every single day, to answer questions your own computer could answer at home.

Renting intelligence forever was never the plan. It's just what we all defaulted into.

The Build

Six months. Two GPUs. One question: what if you owned it?

I stopped renting. I built a machine, loaded it with open models, and spent six months — in 18-to-60-hour sessions — building an operating system where the intelligence lives on your hardware. I taught myself to work terminal-only so I could audit every line instead of trusting black boxes.

Qadir OS is the distilled result: the part anyone can run and own. Not a wrapper around someone's API. An actual local brain, on your actual computer, that works with the WiFi off.

I only sell what I have personally verified works as advertised. That is the whole sales pitch.
