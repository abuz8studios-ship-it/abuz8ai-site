5. Prediction-market / Polymarket tester disclaimer

  • Read-only market data. The tester displays public market data from Polymarket's public API via our read-only proxy. We never send anything back to any market.
  • Simulated balances only. The paper-trading simulator stores its pretend balances and positions only in your own browser's localStorage — on your device, not on our servers. Clearing your browser data erases them.
  • No order placement, no wallets. There is no way to place a real order, connect a wallet, deposit, or withdraw anything through this tool.
  • Not affiliated with Polymarket. ABUZ8 LLC is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Polymarket. All Polymarket names and marks belong to their owners.
  • Not financial advice. Nothing in the tester — prices, simulated results, or commentary — is investment or trading advice. Simulated performance does not predict real performance.
  • Prediction markets involve risk and restrictions. Real prediction markets involve real financial risk and may be restricted or unavailable in some jurisdictions, including parts of the United States. It is your responsibility to know and follow the laws where you live.
  • 18+ and educational use only. The tester is intended for adults (18+) for educational and research purposes only.
