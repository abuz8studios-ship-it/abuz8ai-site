How do I receive the files?

When the vault goes on sale, a download link is delivered to the email you purchase with. The vault is packaged as a ZIP organized by pipeline type. Total size is approximately 2–4GB depending on compression.

What license do I get?

Commercial use license. You can use these outputs in client work, your own products, or as reference material. You cannot resell the vault itself or claim the outputs as AI training data for public models without written permission.

Is this different from the ComfyUI Blueprint?

Yes. The Blueprint gives you the workflow JSON files — the recipes. The Vault gives you the outputs those recipes produce — the meals. They're complementary: Blueprint to build, Vault to see what production looks like.

What hardware were these generated on?

Dual NVIDIA RTX 5090, Ryzen 9950X, 128GB DDR5. Running ComfyUI with CUDA 12.x on Windows 11. These are frontier consumer-hardware outputs — not cloud renders or toy demos.
